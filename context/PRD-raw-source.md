# Employee Self-Evaluation Tool PRD (Working Draft)

## Confirmed Inputs (2026-07-08)
- Primary V1 user: Individual contributors
- Review cadence: Quarterly
- Output format: Narrative summary by pillar
- Integrations in V1: None
- Role framework provided: Senior Principal in Client Delivery
- V1 assessment mode: Narrative only (no ratings)
- V1 output goal: Text the IC can copy for self-reflection input
- V1 output structure: Organized by pillar
- V1 drafting workflow: User-provided evidence first, then tool-generated narrative by pillar

- Updated V1 evidence input: Free-form self-review narrative (not entered per pillar)
- Updated V1 mapping behavior: System maps free-form content to relevant pillars/capabilities
- Updated V1 coaching behavior: System identifies weak/under-supported areas and prompts for additional evidence
- Note: This supersedes prior assumptions about per-pillar evidence entry forms

- V1 evidence follow-up UX: Short targeted questions per missing area (no template required)

- V1 user scope: IC only (no manager or People Ops user experience in V1)

- V1 editing capability: IC can directly edit generated narrative in-app
- V1 refinement capability: IC can request additional agentic refinement passes after edits

- V1 persistence: Auto-save enabled with explicit manual save option

- V1 export behavior: Copy to clipboard only (no file download/export integrations)

- V1 weak-evidence handling: Soft prompt only; user can still finalize narrative

- V1 data retention: Keep drafts and narratives until user deletes

- V1 privacy control: User can delete all their data in one click



- Pillars:
  1. Delivery Exceptionally
  2. Grow Expertise
  3. Grow Slalom
  4. Lead

## Pillar Detail: Delivery Exceptionally (complete)

### Capability: Communication and Influence
- Develops clear, concise, and structured written communications.
- Models the ability to read an audience and influence team members and clients to build trust, create accountability, and drive clear decisions.
- Adapts messages for different audiences to ensure alignment and clarity, while effectively defining and conveying client value.


### Capability: Delivery Management

- Responsible for solution and/or overall project outcomes, and/or expands support across multiple projects.

- Coaches and optimizes delivery efficiency, velocity and predictability.

- Manages the impact of scope and prioritization changes on client relationships.

- Identifies and mitigates risks, escalating issues appropriately while ensuring minimal disruption to delivery.

- Optimizes project structures, roles, planning and estimation, and ways of working, driving Delivery Excellence.

### Capability: Value-based Delivery

- Demonstrates the ability to show value at multiple levels (e.g., engagement, partner solutions, broader Slalom offerings).

- Crafts compelling narratives and case studies that highlight the success of engagements and consistently highlights relevant lessons learned that apply to a client's context.

- Mentors junior team members on articulating value, from discussions of ROI to storytelling techniques.


### Capability: Customer Relationship Management

- Expands trusted relationships beyond immediate clients to serve delivery needs.

- Understands client business priorities and challenges, while positioning Slalom as a trusted advisor and introducing the right SMEs.

- Identifies and acts on Customer Love priorities within engagements.


### Capability: Delivery Quality and Impact

- Leverages expertise, Slalom and industry best practices, and emerging technologies to guide teams in developing high-quality solutions.

- Solves complex challenges by applying structured problem-solving, critical analysis, and creative thinking to improve delivery and outcomes.

- Implements innovative thinking and approaches (self and others).

- Guides team in defining and tracking success measures (qualitative and quantitative) and articulating how solution outcomes advance client business goals.

- Effectively leverages emerging technologies (e.g., AI, AI agents) to enhance productivity and delivery outcomes.


## Pillar Detail: Grow Expertise (complete)

### Capability: Associated Roles

- Deepens and/or broadens mastery in Functional / Technical roles (e.g., Advanced+ in 2+).

- Develops in Engagement roles (e.g., Delivery Solution Lead, Engagement Lead).

- Develops in Pursuit Roles (e.g., Sales Solution Lead).

- Develops and upskills as a People Leader.


### Capability: Learning and Development

- Coaches others on Functional / Technical roles and delivery methodologies (e.g., Delivery Excellence).

- Leads with curiosity, proactively deepening current state knowledge and trends related to capability, industry, and/or solutions.

- Supports and/or delivers learning opportunities directly in service of talent needs to encourage rapid learning and skill development.

- Actively develops understanding of capability, solution, and/or industry trends and best practices to support their own work.


### Capability: Knowledge Management

Applies strong knowledge management practices, ensuring self and teams are contributing to and leveraging Slalom's knowledge capital.

### Capability: Solution Enablement

- Shares expertise, market, and/or delivery insights with capability and market solutions leaders to inform existing and/or new Slalom solutions (e.g., perspectives, offerings, accelerators).

## Pillar Detail: Grow Slalom (complete)

### Capability: Business Development and Sales

- Proactively identifies and relays opportunities for Slalom in delivery environments.

- Helps shape deals (e.g., responding to RFPs) and ensure a smooth transition from sales to delivery.

- Contributes to sales solutioning for pursuits, especially in the context of delivery environments.

### Capability: Business Strategy and Planning

- Understands Slalom's business and strategy, especially in context of capability. Proactively reads the market to understand how solutions align to customer needs.

- Shares expertise and delivery learnings with capability and market solutions leaders to inform discipline, sub-capability, and capability planning.

### Capability: Networking and Brand Stewardship

- Intentionally grows and leverages professional network in delivery environments and areas of interest and expertise

- Upholds Slalom's brand in interactions with professional network and client engagements.

### Capability: Business Operations

- Understands key delivery metrics / drivers (e.g., revenue, Customer Love, continuous quality).

- Supports capability operational and financial health (e.g., staffing, utilization, billable hours, team size, bill rates), with a focus on direct reporting team.

- When managing an engagement, effectively oversees project financials and ensures healthy margins by tracking hours and revenue, updating forecasts, ensuring accurate invoicing, and addressing over- or under-burn scenarios.

### Capability: Culture and Community

- Intentionally grows and leverages Slalom network across projects and capability / geography teams; helps others build relationship across communities.

- Encourages others to engage in Slalom events and internal initiatives (e.g., employee resource groups, learning events).

- Shapes Slalom culture by modeling core values on engagements and within teams, creating meaningful connections in both physical and digital environments.

### Capability: Talent Strategy

- Supports talent management processes in discipline, sub-capability, and capability (e.g., talent acquisition, onboarding, staffing, performance management, engagement, retention).

## Pillar Detail: Lead (complete)

### Capability: Career Development

- Understands team member career aspirations, interests, skills, and skill gaps.

- Actively supports team member career development planning and helps tailor development opportunities.

### Capability: Collaboration

- Effectively regulates and modifies communications and behaviors to collaborate with others.

- Navigates organizational dynamics.

- Focuses on solutions to resolve challenges, and respectfully prompts productive conflict to achieve the best result.

### Capability: Performance Management

- Ensures directs understand performance expectations and helps leaders within people tree set and uphold performance expectations.

- Proactively identifies early signs of performance gaps, ensuring leaders within people tree take action on concerns, and drives accountability.

- Assesses directs' performance against defined expectations and outcomes.

- Contributes insights in calibration discussions and drives consistency in team performance evaluation and differentiation.

### Capability: Vision and Strategy

- Ensures collective accountability for team strategy by providing clear direction.

- Proactively shares team updates to build awareness and support connection to Slalom priorities.

### Capability: Change Leadership

- Manages expectations both up and down with confidence and non-attachment.

- Coaches teams through change, equipping them with tools to navigate uncertainty, build resilience, and sustain performance.

### Capability: Feedback

- Holds self and teams accountable for giving and requesting clear and constructive feedback (e.g., on-demand, performance reviews, end-of-engagement).

- Seeks out constructive feedback, challenging oneself to consider alternatives and maintaining an open mind.

### Capability: Self-Management

- Models self-management, resilience, and accountability for others.

- Prioritizes and proactively manages workload, balancing client and internal activities.

- Reflects to promote a deeper understanding of self and awareness of own agency.

- Proactively addresses growth opportunities and engages in career development planning.
